01 Problem Definition & Motivation
  • SMS OTP Exploits: Susceptible to SIM-swapping, SS7 telecom attacks, and automated reverse-proxy phishing kits (e.g. Evilginx2).
  • Generative Deepfakes: Rapid proliferation of realistic facial and voice synthesis invalidates naive static biometric checks.
  • Indiscriminate Friction: Traditional 2FA interrupts trivial transactions while offering insufficient protection for high-value fund transfers.
Objective: Deliver an adaptive, unreplayable multimodal verification middleware failing-closed against presentation attacks.
02 Context-Aware Risk Engine

Every transaction evaluates four contextual telemetry features to compute an adaptive risk score R ∈ [0.0, 1.0]:

R = 0.45·Value + 0.25·Payee + 0.15·Balance + 0.15·Velocity
  • Low Risk (R ≤ 0.40): Seamless instant passthrough (e.g., ₹5,000 to known payee).
  • High Risk (R > 0.40): Automated interception triggering interactive Look & Speak step-up challenge.

04 Active 3D Spatial Liveness Check

Client-side MediaPipe FaceMesh tracks 468 3D landmarks at 30 fps to evaluate spatial head orientation:

  • Yaw Normalization: Uses temple-to-nose X-axis offset ratio (yawNorm = noseOffset / templeWidth). Unaffected by noisy Z-depth.
  • Dynamic Prompts: System randomly mandates 'Turn Left', 'Turn Right', 'Look Up', or 'Look Down'.
  • Anti-Replay: Requires active motion execution within strict 15-second challenge time window.

05 Acoustic OTP & Whisper ASR
  • Dynamic Passcode: 6-digit random one-time code vocalized by the claimant.
  • OpenAI Whisper ASR: Transcribes spoken audio with real-time digit-by-digit karaoke UI synchronization.
  • Speaker Timbre Verification: Extracts 20-dimensional MFCC vectors and computes cosine distance against enrolled baseline.
  • Acoustic Anti-Spoofing: Analyzes spectral flatness, zero-crossing rate, and rolloff variance to flag cloned audio.

06 KYC Selfie Cross-Verification
  • Ground-Truth Binding: Cross-matches live webcam crop directly against the user's approved eKYC identity document photograph.
  • Photometric Similarity: 3-channel CIE-LAB color histogram comparison (Bhattacharyya distance).
  • Structural Similarity (SSIM): Evaluates grayscale facial structural alignment (Threshold ≥ 0.60 → MATCH).

07 LLM Multimodal Decision Fusion

Google Gemini 3.8 Flash synthesizes all telemetry into an explainable decision:

Telemetry = { Liveness, AudioCode, VoiceTimbre, TextureSpoof, KYC_Face, Risk }
  • ALLOW: Biometrics authenticated and consistent with KYC baseline.
  • DENY: Failed active challenge or spoof pattern detected (fail-closed).
  • ESCALATE: Edge-case ambiguity routed to administrative human review.
